Privacy Policy

This policy explains how DeviceCue handles information in accounts, device verification, community features, feedback, and support, and how you can manage it.

Effective: October 4, 2026. Applies to: DeviceCue 1.0.

Operator and scope

DeviceCue is operated and published by 质感工场（杭州）科技有限公司, a company in Hangzhou, China. You can browse device information and verified owner counts, compare devices, submit ownership verification, join model discussions, and save and sync a wishlist. This policy also covers in-app feedback, support email, and the DeviceCue support website.

The initial release serves general device users in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, and New Zealand; actual availability is shown in the App Store. The operator manually handles support, device verification, community moderation, reports, and personal-information requests.

Contact: developer@txturefty.com. Use this address for help, a review of a decision, or a personal-information request, including when you cannot sign in.

What information we process, and why

Apple account identifier, email, and sign-in session
When you choose Sign in with Apple, we process the account identifier Apple provides, the email you authorize (which may be a private relay address), identity credentials, and necessary session information. These support account creation, verification, security, sync, and handling stored Apple authorization credentials during account deletion. Sign-in requests email, not your name. Authorization-code exchange and revocable credentials are handled server-side.
Wishlist and sync records
Candidate device identifiers, add or remove operations, and sync records and receipts needed to prevent duplicate processing are associated with your account, stored through Supabase, and synced to devices using that account. Your wishlist is not public. Operations without cloud confirmation remain marked as pending sync.
Device records and verification photos
The photos, model, optional note, and review status you submit are associated with your account for manual ownership verification and review requests. Photos are kept in private storage and are not public. Public counts are deduplicated by verified account and model; pending, rejected, or removing records do not count. AI review is not used.
Community topics, replies, reports, and blocks
Topics and replies are associated with your account and become public after approval, using a separate community identifier and system-assigned display name rather than your Apple email. Pending or rejected text and review notes are visible only to you and the service handling them. Report reasons, notes, and blocking relationships support content handling and personal display; they are not disclosed to the reported person. The operator reviews content and reports manually.
In-app feedback
Feedback requires no sign-in and is not automatically linked to your Apple account. Submitting sends your message, an email address if you choose to provide one, the app version and build number, operating-system version, language and region settings, and a random submission identifier. The server records the receipt time and receipt identifier. We use these to investigate problems, improve features, prevent duplicate submissions, and contact you if you provide an email. Feedback is not public. Your message and email may identify you, so provide only necessary information.
Support email
When you email us, we process the contact details, description, attachments, and correspondence you provide to respond, investigate, and handle personal-information requests. Cover unrelated personal information before sending screenshots.
Necessary network and service logs
The network layers of the website and online services receive information such as IP addresses, request times, paths, status, and necessary error details for delivery, operation, and security troubleshooting. The feedback database does not store IP addresses or IP hashes; rate limiting uses global counters. Providers' network and operational logs are separate from feedback records.

This version has no advertising or behavioral analytics features and does not use feedback for targeted advertising. Public browsing, comparison, and feedback need no account. Device verification, publishing community content, wishlists, and cross-device sync require sign-in.

On-device storage, permissions, and external resources

The app stores the usable catalog, account session, that account's wishlist cache, and unsynced operations on the device. Local wishlists are kept separately per account. Signing out hides that account's list; signing back into the same account lets you read it or retry sync. Changes not uploaded cannot be recovered from the cloud after uninstalling the app or losing or damaging the device.

Camera access is requested only when you take a verification photo. Before upload, the image is regenerated to remove original metadata such as location. Photo-library, contacts, location, and microphone access are not needed. Closing the app clears only the local photo draft. A photo uploaded before a failed record submission may remain in private storage; contact support to request cleanup. Successful account deletion also handles that account's verification photos.

Opening a product source takes you to a third-party website operated under its own data practices. DeviceCue's help and privacy pages are static: they contain no analytics scripts, web feedback forms, tracking tools, or automatically loaded third-party resources. GitHub Pages still processes website access information as described below.

Service providers and processing locations

Apple
Provides Sign in with Apple for identity verification and authorization. Apple's own services are covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.
Supabase
Provides account authentication, the database, private photo storage, and server functions for wishlists, device records, community features, and feedback. The project's primary data region is Singapore. Functions may run closer to the person making a request, and service logs and recovery processing follow the provider's arrangements; this does not mean all processing occurs only in Singapore. See the Supabase privacy notice.
GitHub Pages and the DeviceCue website
The public catalog, help, and privacy pages at devicecue.com are hosted and distributed through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process service data in the United States and other countries where it or its providers operate. This static site does not receive verification photos or feedback messages; those use the separate Supabase services. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
Google Workspace
The company support inbox uses Google Workspace to handle email and attachments. We do not limit its storage location to a particular country. See Google Workspace security and privacy information.

The operator is based in Hangzhou, China, and may access relevant information for support, manual review, and request handling. Online services may process information outside your country. Providers process the information needed for their roles; our use is limited to the purposes described here. Contact support for information or related requests.

How long information is kept

Account and associated data
Your account, wishlist, device records, verification photos, review notes, community content, reports, blocks, and necessary operation receipts are kept while the related account or content remains, to provide features and support review. Removing content or deleting the account handles associated records as described below. Sessions and authorization credentials serve sign-in and account management; local unsynced operations remain available for retry.
In-app feedback
Messages, optional emails, version and system information, language and region settings, and submission and receipt identifiers are kept for 180 days, then removed from the feedback database at the next daily cleanup. You may request earlier deletion using your feedback identifier. Feedback is not linked to your account, so deleting an account does not automatically delete feedback.
Support email and attachments
Necessary correspondence is kept while a request is handled and cleared within 180 days after it is resolved. Contact support to request earlier deletion. If information is still needed to investigate an unresolved request, we will explain what is needed and why.
Website access information
GitHub Pages logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security, whether or not visitors are signed into GitHub. GitHub also processes request and service information under its privacy statement. Retention depends on processing purposes and applicable obligations, rather than a fixed website-log period promised by us. We do not maintain our own per-request website logs or have direct control over individual records in GitHub's platform logs. These records are separate from feedback stored in Supabase. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub retention statement.
Provider logs and recovery copies
The current Supabase Free plan gives us access to approximately one day of API and database logs and does not include automatic database backups provided to us. This does not establish that every internal provider log or recovery copy is permanently erased within 24 hours. Records retained by providers for operation, security, or recovery follow their processes and cannot be individually erased immediately through the app.

Deletion depends on a confirmed successful result. We do not promise simultaneous, immediate erasure from every system, offline device, or recovery copy.

Deleting your account and associated data

Open Settings, choose Delete account and data, read the notice, and confirm Permanently delete account and data. Deletion cannot be undone. Signing out or uninstalling the app does not delete the cloud account.

The flow handles the cloud account, wishlist, device records and verification photos, revokes Apple authorization credentials stored by the service, and clears the associated session and account cache on the current device. Follow the app's prompt to check and stop using this app under Apple Account → Sign in with Apple in system Settings; authorizations that may have occurred on other devices also need to be checked.

Account deletion also deletes your topics and replies, community identifier, and reports and blocks you submitted. Deleting a topic deletes all replies in it, including other people's replies; deleting one reply does not affect other replies. Deleted content is no longer public.

If deletion fails or the result is unconfirmed, the app shows an error or Account deletion not yet complete, pauses related sync and changes, and provides a retry action. Wait for a clear success result; submitting a request does not mean deletion is complete. Contact support if you cannot sign in or the problem continues.

You can separately remove a device record and its photo under My Devices. Removing records no longer count toward owner totals, and photo-cleanup failures can be retried. Other offline devices clear their caches after confirming online; you can also uninstall the app on those devices. If cleanup on the current device fails, choose Retry clearing this device's data.

In-app feedback is not linked to an account. To delete it, separately email support with the feedback identifier; you do not need to provide Apple sign-in credentials. Service logs, email, and recovery copies follow the retention arrangements above.

Your personal-information rights and how to contact us

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, a copy, correction, completion, or deletion of personal information, withdraw consent, or ask us to explain processing. Wishlist changes, content removal, and account deletion are available in the app. For other requests or concerns about a decision, email developer@txturefty.com with your request and the relevant account, content, or feedback identifier.

The operator handles requests manually and, when needed, checks only information proportionate to the request's risk to prevent unauthorized disclosure or deletion. Do not send Apple passwords, verification codes, or sign-in credentials. Without an email in your feedback, we cannot contact you directly; you may email us later with the identifier to provide contact details or request deletion.

We handle requests under applicable requirements and explain when more information is needed or a request cannot be fulfilled. You may ask for a review through the same email address if you believe a result is incorrect.

Minors

DeviceCue is for general device users and is not specifically directed to children. This version does not verify age or guardian identity. Minors should follow applicable age and guardian requirements and avoid unnecessary personal information in photos, public discussions, or feedback.

A guardian or other user who finds inappropriate personal information about a minor can email support with the relevant content or feedback identifier. We will investigate and handle deletion requests without asking for unrelated sensitive information.

Policy updates

When data uses, providers, or other important arrangements change, we will update this page and in-app information, and provide notice or obtain consent where applicable. Updated versions will state their effective date, and previous versions will be retained.

This policy takes effect on October 4, 2026. See the user guide for instructions.
