User Guide

See how many people own the same model, record your own devices, and learn about and compare candidates. This guide explains each step, and what to do when something goes wrong.

Applies to: DeviceCue 1.0. Updated: October 4, 2026.

Find and understand a device

In the catalog, enter a brand, a full model name, or a common alias, or filter by category and brand. The catalog currently covers some noise-cancelling over-ear headphones and full-frame interchangeable-lens cameras; categories and models not yet covered are not shown as empty entries.

Can’t find a device

Clear the filters first, then check whether the model name contains a different generation, letter, or number. If there is still no result, the model is not in the current catalog. That does not mean the device does not exist, or that it is not worth considering.

What to read first on a detail page

Start with positioning, key features, suitable scenarios, and main limitations, then check the specifications and conditions against your own use. Confirm that the page describes the exact model and configuration you need — for example, camera body-only and kit listings, or over-ear headphones and same-named earbuds, do not share the same data.

Manufacturer specifications, editorial explanations, and unknown fields are presented separately. Manufacturer figures such as battery life need to be read together with the original test conditions and do not represent everyone’s actual results.

Owner counts and device verification

The catalog and device detail pages show the number of verified owners within DeviceCue, deduplicated by account and model. Repeated submissions of the same model by the same person still count as one; this is not global sales volume or the total number of users.

“Loading” or “Temporarily unavailable” means a reliable result has not been retrieved yet — do not read it as zero. When a refresh fails, the previously kept number is marked as not yet updated and can be reloaded.

  1. Find the exact model you own, open its detail page, and choose “I Own This Device.”
  2. Sign in with Apple, take a photo of the actual device, and confirm the model and appearance are clearly visible; you may add a note of up to 500 characters.
  3. After submitting, check the status under “My Devices.” Entries pending review do not count toward the number until verified; if rejected, you can view the reason and retake the photo.

Verification photos are not public. Submitting a photo requires a network connection; if submission fails, your current photo and note are kept so you can retry. Closing the app clears only the on-device photo draft, so you will need to retake it. If a photo was uploaded but the device record failed to submit, it may remain in private cloud storage. Successful account deletion removes that account’s verification photos; you can also contact support to request help.

Choosing “Remove this device” under “My Devices” removes the record for that model together with its verification photo, and updates the count. You can retry if it fails; signing out does not delete cloud records.

The operator manually reviews device photos. Submitting a photo does not mean it has been approved; check the status in My Devices. If you have a question about a review result, contact the support email.

Same-model discussions

From a specific model’s detail page, enter “Discussions” to browse topics. Once signed in, you can start topics about usage questions, usage experiences, accessory pairing, or troubleshooting, and reply to published topics. Users without verified devices can also join the conversation.

Submitted text first appears as pending review; it becomes public once approved, and if rejected you can view your own review note. If submission fails, your current text is kept so you can retry; drafts are not saved after you close the editor. A verification badge only reflects ownership status at the time of the query — it does not mean the platform has verified the statement.

In the “content actions” of a topic or reply, you can report inappropriate content or block a user; manage “Blocked users” in the settings menu of the discussion page. A successful report only means it was received, not that it has been handled. Blocking applies to content and reply permissions while signed in; public content remains visible when signed out.

Authors can delete their own content. Deleting a topic deletes all replies in it; deleting a single reply does not affect other replies. Read the confirmation prompt before deleting.

The operator handles moderation and reports manually. To add details to a report or ask about its status, contact the support email and identify the model and content involved.

Compare candidates

  1. On a product detail page, choose “Add to Compare.”
  2. Keep adding candidates from the same category; you can also choose “Add to Compare” from your wishlist.
  3. Open the comparison page. Look first at differences that affect your use, then check the units, configurations, and test conditions of each specification.

Comparison supports 2 to 3 devices in the same category. If you see a notice about mismatched categories or a full list, remove an existing candidate before adding another. Browsing devices and comparing them requires no sign-in.

“No data yet” means the information has not been confirmed — do not treat it as zero or assume it is worse. DeviceCue does not offer overall scores or “best buy” rankings without a reliable basis.

Related picks explain the basis for comparison; they are an entry point for exploring similar devices, not a purchase conclusion that suits everyone.

Save a wishlist

On a product detail page, choose “Add to wishlist.” The first save requires Sign in with Apple, so the list belongs to your account and can be viewed on your other devices. Public browsing and comparison work without an account.

In “Wishlist” you can view, remove, or compare candidates again. Other devices must sign in with the same Apple account; wishlists under different accounts are independent.

When you reopen the app, the local wishlist under the current account remains readable. To confirm the latest choices made on other devices, connect to the network, use “Sync now,” and check that the pending-sync notice disappears.

Sync and offline use

When an account and local records are available, offline changes are saved on this device first and uploaded once the network returns. While “Waiting for cloud confirmation” or an error is shown, you cannot assume other devices have received the change.

“Cloud wishlist not loaded yet”
This device has not yet successfully read the cloud result for this account, so it cannot confirm whether the account has candidates. Check the network and choose “Sync now,” or keep browsing products first.
“No candidates on this device”
There are currently no candidates on this device. Add products, or use “Sync now” to check choices made on other devices.
Operations waiting for cloud confirmation
Keep the app and local data, and retry syncing after the network recovers. First confirm both devices use the same account; do not try to fix sync issues by uninstalling the app.
Unable to read the local wishlist
Use “Retry reading this device’s wishlist.” If the problem persists, keep the error message and contact support; do not clear data or mistake a read failure for an empty wishlist.

Two devices changed the same device

The final result takes effect in the order the server receives and successfully processes the operations; it cannot be determined simply by when you tapped on each device.

For example: device A adds a pair of headphones while offline, and device B removes it while online. When A later connects and uploads, the later-received “add” may bring the headphones back into the list. Sync before continuing on another device, and afterwards check that the list matches your current intent.

Local saving is not cloud backup. After uninstalling the app or losing or damaging the device, operations not yet successfully uploaded cannot be recovered from the cloud. A network failure is never shown as a successful sync.

Catalog updates and gaps

If a catalog update fails, the app keeps the existing usable data; note the source, verification date, and update notice, and retry after the network recovers. On first offline use you can read the built-in verified data, but offline content may not be current at this moment.

If a model in your wishlist is temporarily removed from the catalog, your choice is kept with a note that data for this model is currently unavailable. Once the data is restored, you can continue viewing details and comparisons; it is not automatically replaced with another model.

Exact models are currently identified in text; product photos whose usage rights have not been verified are not shown. If you find an incorrect specification, please record the specific model, field, and official source so it can be checked.

Sign out or delete your account

Sign out

Open “Settings” and choose “Sign out.” Signing out hides this account’s local wishlist but does not delete the cloud account. Unsynced choices remain only on this device; after signing back into the same account you can retry syncing.

Permanently delete account and data

  1. Open “Settings,” and under “Account & Sync” choose “Delete account and data.”
  2. Read the deletion notice, then choose “Permanently delete account and data” to confirm.
  3. Wait for a clear result; if it fails or shows “Account deletion not yet complete,” retry deletion in Settings.

Deletion processes the cloud account, the wishlist, device records and verification photos, revokes Apple authorization credentials stored by the service, and clears the account cache associated with the current device. Deletion cannot be undone. Wishlist sync and device-record changes are paused while deletion is in progress; signing out or uninstalling the app is not a substitute for cloud account deletion.

After deletion, follow the app’s prompt to stop using this app in system Settings under “Apple Account → Sign in with Apple.” The app first handles the stored authorization credentials; authorizations that may have occurred on other devices still need to be checked in system settings.

Other offline devices cannot learn the cloud deletion result immediately; they clean up after confirming online. You can also uninstall the app on that device to clear its local cache. If the current device reports a cleanup failure, choose “Retry clearing this device’s data” in Settings.

Service logs, support email, feedback, and recovery copies follow the privacy policy's retention arrangements. Account deletion does not automatically delete feedback that is not linked to your account; use its Feedback ID to request deletion.

If deletion fails or a successful result has not been confirmed, do not assume the account has been deleted. Retry in the app; if you cannot sign in or the problem continues, contact the support email.

Send feedback or contact support

Send feedback in the app

  1. Open Settings → Send Feedback. Sign-in is not required.
  2. Describe the problem or suggestion. Email is optional; include it if you would like us to contact you.
  3. Tap the send button and wait for confirmation. After success, keep the Feedback ID; touch and hold it to copy it for follow-up or a deletion request.

Feedback includes your message, any email you provide, the app version and build number, operating-system version, language and region settings, and a random submission identifier. It is sent to the service and is not automatically linked to your Apple account or shown publicly.

If sending fails, your message and email remain in the current form so you can retry manually. There is no automatic retry, and leaving the form does not save a persistent draft. A success message means the service received the feedback, not that the issue has been resolved. Without an email, we cannot contact you directly.

Feedback is kept for 180 days and removed at the next daily cleanup. To request earlier deletion or add details, email us with the Feedback ID. See the privacy policy for full information.
